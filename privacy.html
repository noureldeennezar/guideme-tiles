The short version. GuideMe helps you discover places and plan trips. We collect only what the app needs to work, we do not sell your personal data, and you can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app (Settings → Security → Delete my account).

3. How we use your data

  • To run the app and the features above.
  • To keep the app safe and to prevent abuse (for example, one person can report an event only once).
  • To understand crashes and use, so we can improve GuideMe.
  • We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it to build profiles for other companies.

4. Who else handles data

We use these service providers. They process data only to provide their service, under their own privacy policies.

  • Google: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Analytics, Crashlytics), Google Sign-In, AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Google Places for place photos and details.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the place data files, this website, and our subscription-verification service.
  • Groq: generates AI text.
  • OpenStreetMap services (map tiles and address search), OpenWeatherMap (weather), and Wikimedia (Wikipedia, Wikidata and Commons text and pictures).

Some of these providers are located outside your country, including in the United States. By using the app you understand that your data may be processed there.

5. How long we keep data

  • Your account data stays until you delete your account.
  • Usage and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period according to the Firebase settings.
  • When you delete your account, we erase your profile and everything listed in section 6 straight away.

6. Your choices and your rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app go to Settings → Security → Delete my account. No app? Use the delete-account page.
  • Turn off usage and crash reports: Settings → Privacy.
  • Change ad consent (EU / UK): Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Location and notifications: switch them off any time in your phone settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or Egypt), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data and to object to some processing. Write to and we will answer within 30 days.

What we keep after you delete your account

  • Events you suggested stay as anonymous community content (they do not contain your name).
  • A small marker that an event was reported by an account (a random id and a time) is kept to stop repeated reports. It carries no name or contact detail.
  • Google keeps its own records of a Play subscription and of purchases. Deleting your GuideMe account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play first (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

7. Children

GuideMe is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with Google Firebase, which protects it with industry-standard security. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone up to date.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
