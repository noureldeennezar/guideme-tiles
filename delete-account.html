Delete your GuideMe account

This page is for the GuideMe app by . Last updated:

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open GuideMe and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Under Security, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm, then sign in once more (with Google or your password) so we know it is you.

Option 2: you cannot open the app

Send an email from the address of your GuideMe account to

Use the subject Delete my GuideMe account. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • Your profile: name, email, phone number and avatar.
  • Your favourites, bookmarks, recently viewed places, recent searches, trips and learned preferences.
  • Your public "I am going" plans in the community, if you made any (when that feature is on).

What is kept

  • Events you suggested stay as anonymous community content (they carry no name).
  • A small anti-abuse marker (a random id and a time) that shows an event was reported by an account. It has no name or contact detail.
  • Records that Google keeps about a Play subscription or purchase.

If you have GuideMe Plus

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → GuideMe, otherwise it may keep renewing.

More detail on how we handle data is in the Privacy Policy.
